ShatterPulse 1.0.0
Created by Schrubber

Blizzard-style Frozen, Shatter and Fingers of Frost proc alerts for Frost Mages in WoW Forever.

WHAT IT SHOWS
- Frozen target: frost arcs on the left and right of your character.
- Fingers of Frost: a separate frost crown above your character.
- Both active: all three indicators can appear together.
- Frozen visuals disappear with the real freeze aura. No guessed Frost Nova timer is used.

VISUALS
- Retail Split Overlay (recommended)
- Split + Ice Lance
- Clean Ice Lance
- Ice Lance + Glow
- Adjustable overlay scale, spacing, opacity, glow, flash speed and flash strength.

SOUNDS
Five bundled sounds are included:
- Crystal
- Ice Crack
- Frost Chime
- Sharp Ping
- Frost Whoosh

The sound can be triggered by freeze procs and, optionally, Fingers of Frost.

INSTALLATION
1. Delete the old IceLanceHelper folder if you used a pre-release version.
2. Copy the ShatterPulse folder to:
   World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns\
3. Start/reload WoW Forever.

MINIMAP
- Left-click: open settings
- Right-click: preview both overlays
- Drag: move the minimap button

TECHNICAL NOTE
WoW Forever restricts some combat and enemy-aura information. ShatterPulse uses Blizzard-safe proc events and AuraContainer tracking instead of restricted combat-log tricks.
