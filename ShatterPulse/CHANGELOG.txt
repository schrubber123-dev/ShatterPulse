ShatterPulse 1.0.0 - Initial Public Release

- Renamed the project from the pre-release name IceLanceHelper to ShatterPulse.
- Frozen target tracking for Frost Nova, Frostbite, Freeze and configured hard-freeze auras.
- Fingers of Frost tracking with a separate top proc indicator.
- Retail-inspired split frost overlay.
- Adjustable glow, opacity, scale, spacing, flash speed and flash strength.
- Optional Ice Lance icon display modes.
- Five bundled frost proc sounds.
- Separate sound toggles for freeze and Fingers of Frost.
- Minimap configuration button.
- Preview modes for Frozen, FoF and Both.
- Blizzard-style settings window with General, Visuals, Sounds, Preview and FAQ / About pages.
- FAQ and diagnostics included.
- Created by Schrubber.
